# Inverge product context

This context is derived from the approved repository documents. The precedence in
[AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md) applies; the PRD, project brief, accepted decisions, and
[brand system](./docs/BRAND_SYSTEM.md) remain authoritative.

## Register

product

The public marketing surface has its own brand register. Administration and
authenticated workspaces use the product register.

## Users

African builders validate ideas and run curated campaigns. Backers review
delivery and support projects. Administrators review campaigns, verification
cases, and account access through clearly attributed actions.

## Product Purpose

Make idea validation and milestone accountability understandable. Show only
confirmed account, payment, and campaign states, with explicit boundaries for
preview data and unavailable services.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, precise, and human. Use concise labels that explain an action and its
consequences. Preserve the established emerald, forest, and warm-paper identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic banking blue, neon crypto styling, speculative investment copy,
decorative dashboard metrics, and unlabelled sample data. Do not manufacture
financial, verification, or authorization outcomes.

## Design Principles

- Keep the main task visible and reveal supporting detail when needed.
- Use shared components and semantic tokens so related screens behave alike.
- Make status, consequences, and recovery actions readable without hover.
- Distinguish preview actions from confirmed backend changes.
- Design for small screens and unreliable connections from the start.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard operation, visible focus, and reduced motion. Preserve
readable contrast, useful loading and error states, long-content reflow, and
44-pixel touch targets. Default backer flows use familiar language.
